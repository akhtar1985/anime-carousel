Data attributes

Every option is available as data- plus the kebab-case option name. Values are coerced against the default's type, so booleans accept "false", numbers are parsed, and objects take JSON. 

<div data-ajc
     data-mode="coverflow"
     data-slides-to-show="3"
     data-space-between="16"
     data-loop="true"
     data-center-mode="true"
     data-autoplay="true"
     data-autoplay-speed="4000"
     data-dots="true"
     data-dots-type="fraction"
     data-draggable="true"
     data-mousewheel="true"
     data-lazy-load="ondemand"
     data-as-nav-for="#thumbs"
     data-responsive='[{"breakpoint":768,"settings":{"slidesToShow":1}}]'>
  …
</div>

Aliases work here too:

data-effect  data-slides-per-view  data-loop  data-centered-slides
data-navigation  data-pagination  data-delay  data-swipe
data-touch-move  data-start-index  data-breakpoints  data-controller

JSON shorthand for anything awkward in HTML:

data-ajc='{"mode":"cards","infinite":true,"slidesToShow":2.5}'

Then either construct manually, or let the plugin collect every marked element: AnimeCarousel.autoInit().
